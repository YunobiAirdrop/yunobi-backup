Scientists call this the holiday paradox. A week of vacation feels slow while it happens, because everything is new. But looking back, it feels huge and full. A month of routine? It flies past, and leaves almost nothing behind. Think about it. Can you name a single Tuesday from three months ago? Probably not. But that one weekend trip to the beach? You remember the smell of the sunscreen. That is your brain's clock, and it only counts what stands out.
