## Naskah (3 beat, ~85 kata, ~34 detik)
[BEAT 1 — hook]
Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the building of the pyramids.
[BEAT 2 — bukti]
The Great Pyramid was finished around 2560 BC. Cleopatra died in 30 BC — over 2,500 years later. The Moon landing was 1969 — barely 2,000 years after her death.
[BEAT 3 — payoff + loop]
To Cleopatra, the pyramids were already ancient ruins — older to her than she is to us. We compress history in our heads. But the past is deeper than you think.
