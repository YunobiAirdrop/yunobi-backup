## Varian hook (3 kategori berbeda)
- A (Curiosity Gap): "Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire."
- B (Specificity & Data): "Teaching began at Oxford in 1096. The Aztec capital was founded in 1325."
- C (Contrarian): "When Oxford opened its doors, the Aztecs didn't exist yet."
TERPILIH: A (klaim paling mengejutkan, 8 kata, langsung)

## Naskah (3 beat, ~90 kata, ~36 detik)
[BEAT 1 — hook]
Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire. Teaching at Oxford began in 1096.
[BEAT 2 — bukti]
The Aztec capital, Tenochtitlan, was founded in 1325 — nearly 230 years later. While students in England were already debating philosophy in stone halls, the island city that would become Mexico City was still a swamp.
[BEAT 3 — payoff + loop]
Oxford had graduated generations of scholars before the first Aztec emperor ever took the throne. We picture the past in the wrong order. Oxford isn't just old — it's older than an entire empire.
