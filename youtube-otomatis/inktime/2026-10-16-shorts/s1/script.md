## Naskah (3 beat, ~95 kata, ~38 detik)
[BEAT 1 — hook]
Napoleon wasn't short. History lied to you. He stood 5 foot 7 inches — taller than the average Frenchman of his time.
[BEAT 2 — bukti]
So where did the myth come from? Two things. First, his Imperial Guard were handpicked giants over 6 feet tall — anyone looks small next to them. Second, British cartoonists drew him tiny to mock him, and the joke stuck for 200 years.
[BEAT 3 — payoff + loop]
The French measured in pouces, the British misread it as inches, and we still believe the cartoon. Napoleon wasn't short. He was propaganda's victim.
