George Sodder had a theory he never abandoned. He believed his children had been taken — before the fire started, or lifted out of the house while it burned. George was an outspoken man who had openly criticized the Italian leader Mussolini, and some in town knew it. He suspected enemies had targeted his family. He hired private investigators. He chased every lead himself. And he raised a billboard on Route Sixteen bearing the faces of his five missing children, offering a reward. That billboard stood for decades.
