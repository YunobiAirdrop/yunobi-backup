The family fought back against the night. George tried to back his coal truck against the house to reach the upper windows. It would not start. It had run fine the day before. Two other family vehicles refused to start as well. The phone line George tried to use had been cut — cleanly, deliberately. A neighbor finally reached the fire department, but the crew arrived hours later. No alert system. No hydrant nearby. By the time water touched the flames, there was almost nothing left to save.
