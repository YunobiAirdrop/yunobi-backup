Here is the detail that will not leave this story alone. In nineteen forty-nine, George had the ground beneath the house excavated, hunting for anything — a bone, a tooth, a button from a child's coat. The dig turned up a few small fragments. A pathologist concluded they were likely animal bone, far too old to belong to the children. Once again: nothing. A fire burning under an hour had supposedly vaporized five human bodies so completely that not even a tooth survived. Fire science says that is not how it works.
