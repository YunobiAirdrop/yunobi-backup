Here the tragedy becomes a mystery. The house burned for only about forty-five minutes. That is not long enough to destroy human bones. Cremation takes hours at temperatures far hotter than a house fire. Forensic experts have said it for decades: a forty-five minute house fire leaves remains. Always. Yet the coroner found nothing in the ashes. No bones, no teeth, no trace of five growing children. The official line was that the fire had simply been hot enough to consume everything. The science never backed that claim.
