In nineteen sixty-seven, over two decades after the fire, Jennie Sodder received a letter with no return address. Inside was a photograph of a young man in his thirties. A handwritten note on the back claimed he was Louis — the boy who had been nine on the night of the fire. Jennie stared at the face. The family hired another investigator. They never confirmed who the man was, or where the photograph came from. But Jennie kept it for the rest of her life, framed. A maybe. A ghost of a son.
