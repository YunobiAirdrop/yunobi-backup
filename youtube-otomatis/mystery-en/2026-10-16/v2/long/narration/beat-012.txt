George Sodder died in nineteen sixty-eight. Jennie died in nineteen eighty-nine. Neither ever learned what happened to their children. The billboard on Route Sixteen eventually came down. The house was never rebuilt. But the case never fully died. It lives on in books, documentaries, and late-night conversations — because it breaks something basic in us. A fire leaves remains. That is how the world works. Unless, on one Christmas Eve in West Virginia, someone made certain it would not.
