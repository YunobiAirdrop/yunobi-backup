Look at what the authorities did — or failed to do. The fire chief told George the blaze had been hot enough to destroy everything, bone included. But no thorough search of the ashes was ever conducted. When a small bone fragment surfaced years later, testing suggested it did not belong to any of the children. The investigation was brief. The case was closed fast. George believed the local authorities wanted it buried. Whether that was corruption, incompetence, or just the limits of a small-town department in nineteen forty-five, the result was identical: no answers.
