A house burned to the ground in forty-five minutes. Five children were trapped inside. But when the ashes cooled, there were no bones. Not a single one. Christmas Eve, nineteen forty-five. A family of twelve in Fayetteville, West Virginia, asleep in their home. By one in the morning, the house was an inferno. Four children and both parents escaped into the freezing night. The other five never came out. And when investigators sifted the ashes, they found nothing. No skulls. No teeth. Nothing at all.
