Then came the strange events before the fire. Months earlier, a man came to the house asking about work. He studied the fuse box and remarked it would cause a fire someday. Another stranger once pointed at the house and said the children would be destroyed. A wrong-number call woke the house at half past midnight. The phone line was cut. The ladder vanished. The trucks would not start. Any one of these could be coincidence. All of them together feel like a plan.
