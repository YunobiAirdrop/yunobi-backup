Today, the case is known as the American Dyatlov Pass, named for the nine hikers who died under impossible circumstances in the Ural Mountains in nineteen fifty-nine. The parallels are chilling. A group, a remote wilderness, decisions that defy logic, and answers that never came. The families of the Yuba County Five never got closure. Four mothers buried their sons. One mother never even got that. Nearly fifty years later, the files remain open, the questions remain unanswered, and somewhere in the Plumas National Forest, the truth about what happened on the night of February twenty-fourth is still waiting. Five friends went to a basketball game. Only four ever came out of the mountains.
