The search for the five men was enormous. At the time, it was one of the largest missing-person searches California had ever seen. Hundreds of volunteers, sheriff's deputies from multiple counties, the National Guard, helicopters sweeping the ridgelines. The families were relentless. The boys' mothers gave interviews, pleading for anyone with information to come forward. They knew their sons. These were not men who would abandon a car and wander into the wilderness. They were gentle, trusting, creatures of routine. Every lead was chased. Every tip was followed. And for three months, the mountain gave up nothing. No footprints that led anywhere. No clothing. No sign that five men had ever walked up that road at all.
