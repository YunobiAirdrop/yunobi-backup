To understand what happened, you need to know who these men were. The five were Bill Sterling, twenty-four, Jack Huett, twenty-four, Ted Weiher, thirty-two, Jack Madruga, thirty, and Gary Mathias, twenty-five. Friends and family called them "the boys." All five had mild intellectual disabilities or psychiatric conditions, and they had bonded over it, playing on the same basketball team, spending nearly every day together. Gary Mathias was the exception in one way. He had served in the army, he was on psychiatric medication, and without it, those close to him said, he could become unpredictable. He had been doing well, though. He had a job. He had friends. On that Friday night, all five piled into Jack Madruga's nineteen sixty-nine Mercury and headed north to the game.
