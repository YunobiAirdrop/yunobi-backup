And this is the part that haunts investigators to this day. The trailer was stocked. There was enough military-style food inside to feed all five men for a year. There was a heating system, with fuel. Ted Weiher could have stayed warm. He could have eaten. But he barely touched the food. Only a few rations had been opened, and clumsily, as if by someone who didn't know how. The heater had not been used properly. He was found wrapped in sheets on the bed, wearing light clothing, his shoes gone. And in the trailer, investigators found a pair of sneakers. They belonged to Gary Mathias. But Gary Mathias was nowhere to be found. Neither his body, nor any trace of him, has ever been discovered.
