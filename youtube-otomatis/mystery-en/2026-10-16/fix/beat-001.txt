Five men went to a basketball game in 1978. They never made it home. And their car was found nineteen miles up a mountain road, in the exact opposite direction. On the night of February twenty-fourth, nineteen seventy-eight, five friends from Yuba City, California drove to Chico to watch a college basketball game. They were supposed to drive straight home after. Fifty miles south. Instead, their car was discovered days later, abandoned on a remote logging road high in the Plumas National Forest, northeast of everything they knew. The car was undamaged. It had gas. There were no signs of a struggle. And the five men were gone. What happened on that mountain has never been explained. This is the story of the Yuba County Five.
