The game itself was unremarkable. UC Davis versus Chico State. The boys watched it, and witnesses remembered them there. Afterward, they stopped at a convenience store in Chico, buying a large amount of snacks and drinks, enough for the drive home. That was the last confirmed sighting. They never arrived in Yuba City. When they didn't come home, their families grew worried fast, because the boys were creatures of habit. They always came home. By the next day, police were searching. And on February twenty-eighth, four days after they vanished, a Forest Service ranger found the Mercury parked on Oroville-Quincy Highway, a winding mountain road deep in the forest. The car was nineteen miles from the main highway, at an elevation where snow still sat on the ground.
