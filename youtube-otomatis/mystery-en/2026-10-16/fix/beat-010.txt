Then there is the enigma of Gary Mathias. Of the five, he was the one whose mind worked differently in a way that mattered. He took psychiatric medication, and friends said that without it, he could become confused, even paranoid. His sneakers were found inside that trailer, which means at some point, Gary Mathias was there. He took off his shoes, and then he left, or was taken, into the freezing wilderness, and was never seen again. Stranger still, items were missing from Ted Weiher's body. His watch, a treasured gift, was gone. A gold necklace he always wore was gone. His own sneakers were never found. Were these the signs of a struggle? A robbery? Or the confused actions of men in crisis?
