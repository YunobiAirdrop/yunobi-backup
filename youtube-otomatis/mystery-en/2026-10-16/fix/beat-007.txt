In the days after Weiher was found, searchers located the others. The remains of Jack Madruga and Bill Sterling were discovered about eleven miles from where the car had been left, partially scattered by animals. Two days later, the remains of Jack Huett were found two miles from the trailer. All had died of exposure, or its consequences. But Gary Mathias, the one whose sneakers were sitting in the trailer, the one with the psychiatric history, the one whose medication he may not have taken that night, was never found. Not a bone, not a piece of clothing beyond those sneakers. Five men drove up that mountain. Four were found dead. One vanished completely, as if the wilderness had simply swallowed him.
