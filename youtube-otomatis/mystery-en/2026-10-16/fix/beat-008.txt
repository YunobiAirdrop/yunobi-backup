So what happened on that mountain? The theories have never stopped. Some believe Gary Mathias suffered a psychiatric episode and led his friends up the mountain, or that the group was fleeing something, or someone, they saw on the road. Others point out that the boys' intellectual disabilities may have left them unable to cope once the car stopped, unable to use the trailer's supplies, unable to save themselves. But none of it explains why they drove up there in the first place. Why abandon a working car? Why did Weiher survive for weeks and barely eat? And where is Gary Mathias? Nearly fifty years later, the Yuba County Five remains one of America's most unsettling unsolved mysteries. Five friends went to a basketball game. Only four ever came out of the mountains.
