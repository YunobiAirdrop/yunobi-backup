For weeks, search teams combed the mountains. Helicopters, dogs, hundreds of volunteers. Nothing. The snow melted. Spring came. And still, no trace of the five men. Then, in early June, more than three months after they disappeared, a group of motorcyclists rode up to the Daniel Zink campground, at the far end of that same mountain road. There, in a Forest Service trailer, they made a discovery that turned the case from strange to deeply disturbing. On a bed inside the trailer lay the body of Ted Weiher. He had lost eighty to one hundred pounds. His feet were severely frostbitten. His beard had grown long. Ted Weiher had not died that night in February. He had survived for weeks, possibly months, alone in that trailer.
