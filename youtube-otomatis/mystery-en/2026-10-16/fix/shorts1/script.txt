Two men climbed a hill in Brazil in 1966. They never came down alive. When their bodies were found, both were wearing lead masks over their eyes. Like something out of a science experiment. Beside them, a notebook with a cryptic message about protecting their faces. No signs of violence. No poison found. No cause of death, ever. What were they doing up there? And who told them to wear those masks? The Lead Masks Case remains one of the strangest unsolved deaths in history.
