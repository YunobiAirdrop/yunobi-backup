The theories have never stopped coming. Some investigators believe the men witnessed something on the road that night, something that frightened them badly enough to flee up the mountain. Others think Gary Mathias suffered a psychiatric episode and convinced, or forced, his friends to drive away from home. A darker theory says they were run off the road, or met with foul play, and the car was staged. But each theory breaks against the facts. If they were fleeing, why park the car so neatly? If Mathias was in crisis, why did Weiher survive for weeks without eating the food around him? And if there was foul play, where is the evidence? Where is Gary Mathias? The mountain keeps its secrets.
