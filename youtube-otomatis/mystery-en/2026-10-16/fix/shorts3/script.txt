In the eleven hundreds, villagers in Woolpit, England found two children at the edge of a field. A brother and sister. Their skin was green. They spoke no language anyone recognized. They refused all food except raw beans. The boy soon died, but the girl survived, slowly lost her green color, learned English, and told a story no one could verify. She said they came from a land where the sun never shone. Were they lost? Was it illness? Or did they come from somewhere else entirely?
