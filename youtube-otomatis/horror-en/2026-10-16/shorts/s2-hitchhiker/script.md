### beat-001
A driver picked up a hitchhiker on a deserted road at midnight. A young woman in a white dress, shivering. He lent her his jacket and drove toward the address she whispered.
**[ON SCREEN: SHE VANISHED FROM A LOCKED CAR]**
**IMAGE PROMPT:** Deserted country road at midnight, car headlights cutting through fog onto a young woman in a white dress standing at the roadside, cinematic horror, cold blue tones, mist, film grain.

### beat-002
She barely spoke, just stared out the window at the passing trees. When they reached the house, he turned to tell her they had arrived. The back seat was empty. The doors were still locked from the inside.
**IMAGE PROMPT:** Interior of a car at night seen from the driver's seat, empty back seat reflected in the rearview mirror, a dark damp patch on the cushion where someone sat, streetlight glow through windows, eerie stillness.

### beat-003

**IMAGE PROMPT:** Old weathered house at night, warm porch light, an elderly woman in the doorway with a pale shocked expression speaking to a man, dark twisted trees behind them, horror film still, tension.

### beat-004
"That was my daughter Anna," the woman whispered. "She died on that road twenty years ago tonight." At the cemetery, draped over a gravestone marked Anna, lay his jacket. Cold. Wet. Folded neatly.
**IMAGE PROMPT:** Old cemetery at night in thick fog, a weathered gravestone with the name ANNA, a man's jacket draped neatly over it, wet and cold, pale moonlight through fog, chilling final shot, cinematic horror.
