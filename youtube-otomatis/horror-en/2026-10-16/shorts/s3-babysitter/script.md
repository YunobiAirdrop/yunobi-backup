## NARRATION SCRIPT

### beat-001
A babysitter laughed at the creepy prank caller. The phone had rung at midnight, a strange voice whispering: "Have you checked the children?" Just a prank, she thought, and hung up.
**[ON SCREEN: THE CALL CAME FROM INSIDE]**
**IMAGE PROMPT:** Cozy suburban living room at night, a teenage babysitter answering a vintage landline phone, warm lamp glow, a dark staircase rising behind her into blackness, subtle menace, horror film still.

### beat-002
Ten minutes later, it rang again. Same voice, colder now: "Have you checked the children?" Her smile faded. She locked the front door and checked every window.
**IMAGE PROMPT:** Close-up of a teenage girl's frightened face lit by cold phone-screen light in a dark living room, her hand turning a front-door deadbolt lock, night outside the window, tension, cinematic horror.

### beat-003
The third call came at half past twelve. The voice was not whispering anymore. It breathed right into the receiver: "Have you checked the children?" Shaking, she called the police. "Keep him talking," the dispatcher said. "We are tracing it."
**IMAGE PROMPT:** Extreme close-up of an old telephone receiver lying off the hook on a side table, dark living room, a shadowy human silhouette barely visible at the top of a dark staircase in the background, dread, horror.

### beat-004
Sixty seconds of silence. Then the dispatcher came back, voice trembling: "The call is coming from inside the house. Get out now." She grabbed the children and ran, as a man's silhouette watched from the upstairs window, holding a kitchen knife.
**IMAGE PROMPT:** Night exterior of a suburban house, a teenage girl sprinting across the front lawn carrying two small children, in a lit upstairs window a dark male silhouette holding a long knife, porch light halo, terrifying climax, cinematic.
