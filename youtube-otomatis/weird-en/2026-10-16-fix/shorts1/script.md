# Short 1 — The Bloop
Judul: The Ocean Screamed LOUDER Than a Blue Whale #shorts #weirdfacts #foryou
Hook on-screen: LOUDER THAN A BLUE WHALE?!
Hook variants: A) "In 1997, the entire Pacific Ocean screamed — and nobody knows what made the sound." B) "This was the loudest sound ever recorded underwater. It took scientists 8 years to guess what it was." C) "Something in the Pacific made a sound louder than a blue whale. It was heard 3,000 miles away."

### beat-001
In 1997, the entire Pacific Ocean screamed — and nobody knew what made the sound. Microphones three thousand miles apart picked it up at the same time.
**[ON SCREEN: dark ocean depths, hydrophone cables, mysterious blue glow, documentary]**

### beat-002
It was several times louder than a blue whale — the loudest animal on Earth. For years, scientists wondered: was it something alive down there? Something enormous?
**[ON SCREEN: giant blue whale silhouette next to tiny human diver for scale, deep blue water, documentary photo]**

### beat-003
Eight years later, they settled on an answer: icequakes. Icebergs the size of cities, cracking apart in Antarctica. Probably true.
**[ON SCREEN: massive Antarctic iceberg fracturing, giant cracks splitting white-blue ice, aerial documentary photo]**

### beat-004
But for eight years, the loudest sound the ocean ever made had no explanation at all. Follow for stranger facts.
**[ON SCREEN: sound waves rippling through dark deep ocean, sonar visualization, cinematic]**
