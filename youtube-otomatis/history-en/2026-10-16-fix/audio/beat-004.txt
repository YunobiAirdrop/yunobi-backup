The cannon is built by a Hungarian engineer named Orban. Here is the twist nobody expects: Orban first offered his services to the Greeks inside Constantinople. They wanted the cannon — but they couldn't afford his price. So Orban walked out of the city, straight into the Ottoman camp, and Mehmed gave him everything he asked for: unlimited bronze, unlimited money, unlimited men. The result is a monster twenty-seven feet long, cast in two pieces that screw together. It fires stone balls weighing two hundred and seventy kilograms — heavier than three grown men — over a full mile. It takes sixty oxen and two hundred men just to drag it the hundred and fifty miles to the walls. When it fires, the roar is heard ten miles away, and the crew sprints for cover, because the barrel might explode and kill them all. Mehmed doesn't care. He has the biggest gun on the planet. And he is pointing it at the oldest wall on Earth.
