In 1453, a 21-year-old dragged SEVENTY warships over a hill. In ONE night. The city of Constantinople had blocked his fleet with a giant iron chain across the harbor. So Sultan Mehmed greased a road of wooden logs over the hills behind the city... and hauled his entire navy across dry land. The defenders woke at dawn to enemy warships floating INSIDE their safe harbor. The chain was useless. Fifty-three days later, the thousand-year-old city fell. Seventy ships. One night. Zero mountains spared.
