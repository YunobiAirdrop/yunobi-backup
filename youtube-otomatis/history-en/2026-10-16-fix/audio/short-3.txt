These walls survived ONE THOUSAND years. They fell in 53 days. The Theodosian Walls of Constantinople: three layers of stone, a sixty-foot moat, towers every hundred and eighty feet. Never breached in a millennium. Then came Mehmed the Second — twenty-one years old, eighty thousand men, and a cannon the world had never seen. April sixth to May twenty-ninth, 1453. The Middle Ages ended at the breach. A thousand years of stone. Fifty-three days of cannon.
