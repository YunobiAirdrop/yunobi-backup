It's one in the morning, May twenty-ninth, fourteen fifty-three. Outside the greatest walls on Earth, war drums are rolling, and eighty thousand men are moving into position. For a thousand years, these walls have never been broken. Persians, Arabs, Bulgars, Crusaders — armies from three continents have thrown themselves at this stone and died in the moat below. Tonight, a twenty-one-year-old boy is about to do what no emperor, no khan, no crusader ever could. By sunrise, the last city of Rome will fall, its emperor will be dead in the streets, and the Middle Ages will end with it. But to understand how a teenager broke the unbreakable... we have to go back fifty-three days, to the morning the biggest cannon on Earth fired its first shot.
