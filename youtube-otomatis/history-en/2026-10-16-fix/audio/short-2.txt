This cannon needed SIXTY oxen just to move it. In 1453, an engineer named Orban built the biggest gun on Earth for a 21-year-old sultan: twenty-seven feet of bronze, firing stone balls weighing two hundred and seventy kilograms — over a mile. When it fired, the crew RAN for cover, because the barrel might explode. It hammered the thousand-year walls of Constantinople day and night... until the walls broke. Sixty oxen. One cannon. Zero chance for the defenders.
