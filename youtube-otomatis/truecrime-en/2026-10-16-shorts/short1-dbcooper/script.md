## Naskah final (dibaca kata per kata oleh TTS)

In 1971, a man jumped from a Boeing 727 with 200,000 dollars strapped to his body. They never found him.

His name was D.B. Cooper. On a November night, he boarded a flight to Seattle, ordered a bourbon, and calmly told the flight attendant he had a bomb in his briefcase. He demanded 200,000 dollars and four parachutes. The airline paid.

In Seattle, he let the passengers go, then ordered the crew back into the sky. Somewhere over the dark forests of the Pacific Northwest, he strapped the money to his body, lowered the rear stairs, and jumped into the night.

The FBI searched for decades. They found a few rotting bills on a riverbank. But D.B. Cooper? Never found. Dead or alive.

The only unsolved skyjacking in American history.
