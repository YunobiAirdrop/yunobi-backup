## Naskah final (dibaca kata per kata oleh TTS)

Half a billion dollars in art. Stolen in 81 minutes. Never recovered.

March 18th, 1990. Boston. Two men dressed as police officers knocked on the door of the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum. The guards let them in. Big mistake.

In 81 minutes, the thieves cut 13 masterpieces from their frames — including a Vermeer and three Rembrandts — worth over 500 million dollars. They vanished into the night.

No alarms stopped them. No cameras caught their faces.

Today, the empty frames still hang on the museum walls — a promise that the art will return. Nobody has ever been arrested.

The paintings are still out there. Somewhere.
