## Naskah final (dibaca kata per kata oleh TTS)

Five children vanished from a Christmas Eve fire. No bodies. No bones. Nothing.

Christmas Eve, 1945. Fayetteville, West Virginia. The Sodder family's house caught fire in the middle of the night. Five of their ten children were trapped upstairs. Or so everyone thought.

When the fire died, there were no bodies. No bones. Nothing.

The family insisted the fire was set — to cover a kidnapping. Years later, they received a photo of a young man who looked exactly like one of their missing sons.

The FBI closed the case. But the Sodders never believed it. A billboard outside their home read: "What happened to our children?"

Five children. Vanished. Christmas Eve. Still unsolved.
